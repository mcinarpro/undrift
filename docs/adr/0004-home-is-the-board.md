# Home is the Board

Home became the Board on 2026-11-04 – a Lane per Project in Focus, its Issues as Notes moving Ready,
Today, Doing, the other Projects on the Shelf – because the page before it had no surface for the
day's work: with nothing half-done it showed an Empty State and hid Focus, and its right column
listed every Project's Issues unranked, so Mehmet opened it and could not tell what Undrift was
for. The Board puts what he meant to do (Focus, Today) beside what happened (Sessions, commits, Issues
closed), which is the Drift made visible. ADR 0001's five-second answer stands: Up Next, the line
above the Board, leads with the newest Loose End, a Loose End is a flagged Card in Doing and a *Left
off* mark on the Shelf, and the greeting keeps the counts. ADR 0002 stands too: Today and Doing are
selections of GitHub Issues by URL, hold no text of their own, and empty as Issues close – Undrift
still writes nothing to GitHub. ADR 0003 is departed from in one place: the Board's paper has colour,
a pad per Lane in Focus order, because paper is the Board's material – it classifies nothing, and no
other part of the interface takes a colour from it.
