# Areas are gone, and the Graph keeps one hub

Undrift filed Projects under Areas – a theme with a colour, chosen on the Project page since
2026-11-03 – and the same day they were taken out, because they decided nothing: not the Loose
Ends, not Focus, not the Issues, not Needs You. They gave a tint to avatars, a filter to the
Projects rail and the Graph its hubs, for a picker, a dialogue, a write to `config.json` and a
page of design. The one real link between Projects – a post and a short of the same name in two
repositories – was a shared colour and nothing more. The Graph stays, on a single hub, because
it still draws what a list cannot: which Projects have Sessions, and which of those are alive.
Bringing Areas back would mean a use that drives a decision, not a colour.
