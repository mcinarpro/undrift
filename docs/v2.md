# Undrift v2 – the one app to plan the day and work in it

Written 2026-11-05, after a day of design with Claude. v2 restarts the pages from the shell commit
`aa5fbc0`, keeps five later commits (the header with tabs, the Windows terminal, the window state,
the Linux pinch fix, the release workflow) and draws every screen in mock data first
(`src/mocks/day.ts`). The bridge fills them only once they have been used and kept.

## Why this one gets finished

Mercator, Sentinel, Undrift v1: the same cockpit, started again each time, never used daily. The
design blocked; Geist ended that. So the rules are:

1. **Finished means used every day**, not every feature present.
2. **v1 is four things**: Today as a planner, the integrated terminal, the hub sync, the global
   capture. Nothing else until they are used.
3. **Used from day 3**, even rough – Undrift is built inside Undrift.
4. **Two weeks without a new feature**: what bothers goes to the inbox; only that gets fixed.
5. **No new tool repository, ever.** An idea becomes a module or an Issue here. Sentinel and
   Mercator are quarries, not projects.

## The method it serves

- **One Project at a time** – the **Sprint**, 5 days. Hyperfocus is welcome; it has edges.
- **Habits** – English, reading – get a fixed, short slot every day, and a week of squares.
- **Later** holds every new idea until the Sprint ends; it cannot be started from there.
- **The day is timeboxed**, Sunsama's way: tasks are pulled into the day, then given a slot. What is
  not done moves to tomorrow or Later – never lost.
- **Home, garden, family** live in Home Sweet Plan, shared with Mehmet's wife. Undrift may show
  those tasks on the day later; it does not hold them.
- **The system keeps itself**: the Reprise card is drafted by Claude when a session ends, the
  inbox sort is proposed – Mehmet approves in a click. No ritual depends on remembering it.

## Build, after two weeks with the mocks

1. **The terminal**: port Sentinel's `services/pty.rs`, `services/terminal.rs` and
   `modules/terminal/` (portable-pty 0.9 – ConPTY on Windows, openpty on Linux – and xterm.js 6).
   Mehmet adds the dependencies: `cargo add portable-pty@0.9`,
   `bun add @xterm/xterm @xterm/addon-fit @xterm/addon-search @xterm/addon-webgl
   @tauri-apps/plugin-clipboard-manager`.
2. **The hub**: read and write the day, the Projects and the inbox in `mcinarpro/core`; pull at
   launch, every 15 minutes and before a write; never resolve a conflict silently.
3. **The boards**: a Project's post-its from its GitHub Project's Status, read-only through `gh`.
4. **Capture**: `C` in the window, `Ctrl Alt Space` from anywhere (`bun tauri add global-shortcut`).
5. **Claude proposes, Undrift applies**: `claude -p` on a click only – draft the card, sort the
   inbox – with read-only tools; Undrift writes after Mehmet approves. The unmodified `claude`
   binary, his own plan, no credential ever read (Anthropic's legal and compliance page, read
   2026-11-05).

`GLOSSARY.md`, `DESIGN.md` and `README.md` still describe v1; they are rewritten with step 2, when
the words stop moving.
