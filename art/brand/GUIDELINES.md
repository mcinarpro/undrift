# undrift – logo guidelines

Brief: calm and anchored – a place to land, not a dashboard that shouts. Readable at 16 px, works in
one colour, clear of developer-tool clichés (terminal prompts, brackets, rockets).

## 1. The logo

- **Idea**: a line winds in and runs straight down into a dot – the drift ends, you land.
- **Versions**: horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol · wordmark. The wordmark is drawn, not
  typed: its i carries the symbol's dot, and the f and t share one crossbar.
- **Masters** (`art/`): `undrift-symbol.svg`, `undrift-symbol-small.svg`, `undrift-wordmark.svg`,
  `undrift-horizontal.svg`, `undrift-stacked.svg`. Every variant lives in `art/export/`.
- **Geometry** is generated by `art/tools/` (`drop.py` for the symbol, `wordmark.py`, `lockups.py`).
  Change a measurement there and regenerate rather than editing path data by hand.

## 2. Clear space

Keep a clear zone of one dot diameter around the logo on every side. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen |
|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide |
| Symbol | 16 px – below 48 px use `undrift-symbol-small.svg` (heavier line, bigger dot) |

## 4. Colour

The logo has no brand colour. It is black on light surfaces and white on dark ones; in the
interface it takes the text colour (`DESIGN.md`).

The desktop app icon is the symbol master in white with no tile, filling 88 % of its canvas;
the web icons keep it on a `#111111` tile. The tray icon is
`undrift-symbol-tray.svg` (a lighter 22 line) in white with no tile, drawn on the panel itself.

## 5. Typography

The wordmark is custom lettering. The app's typefaces are set in `DESIGN.md`.

## 6. Keep it whole

Use the files as they are: same proportions, the palette above, upright, flat colour, the lockups as
drawn, on a calm background.
